Glint Photo Booth — Support

Questions, bugs or feature ideas? Email essentialdevteam@gmail.com. We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

Leaving guest mode

Hold the faint ⚙︎ in the top-left corner of the booth for 1.5 seconds, then enter your operator PIN. Forgot it? Tap Forgot PIN? and confirm with the iPad's passcode or Face ID.

Printing

Any AirPrint printer on the same Wi-Fi works, including DNP, Mitsubishi and Canon Selphy. Choose it in Devices.

Apple TV or iPad photo wall

Open Glint Wall on the Apple TV (or choose Photo wall on a spare iPad), then on the booth iPad go to the event › Sharing › Connect Apple TV and scan the code.

Glint Pro, Event pass and cancelling

The free plan has every feature with a trial mark on photos and 25 photos a day. Glint Pro (monthly or yearly) and the 7-day Event pass remove both. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Deleting your data

Delete guests, events or your whole Glint account in the app. See the privacy policy for details.
