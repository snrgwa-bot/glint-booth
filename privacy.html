Privacy Policy — Glint Photo Booth

Effective date: September 30, 2026 · Applies to Glint Photo Booth (iPad), Glint Remote (iPhone) and Glint Wall (Apple TV)

Summary: Glint is a photo booth for events. Photos are shared with guests through a private online gallery run by us; guest phone numbers and emails stay on the booth iPad. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Online photos are deleted automatically after 30 days (or 60/90 days on Glint Pro if the operator chooses).

This policy describes how Essential Dev Team ("we," "us") handles information when you use the Glint apps. It covers two kinds of people: the operator who runs the booth, and guests who take photos at an event.

1. Information stored only on the booth iPad

Session photos, guest names, phone numbers, email addresses, survey answers and marketing opt-ins are stored on the operator's iPad. When a guest asks for their photos by text or email, the iPad's own Messages or Mail app sends them — we never receive those contact details. The operator can delete guests, turn on automatic deletion of guest contacts after each event, and choose how long photos stay on the iPad (30, 60 or 90 days).

2. Online sharing (photos and event galleries)

When online sharing is on, booth photos, GIFs and boomerangs are uploaded to our service (hosted on Cloudflare) so guests can open them from a QR code on any phone. Each photo and event gallery has a long random link that cannot be guessed; galleries are only visible to people who have the link. Guests can also upload their own photos to an event wall from their phone; those uploads may be checked by an automated safety filter (Cloudflare Workers AI) before they are shown. Guests can remove their own uploads from the same phone.

Online photos, reactions and comments are deleted automatically when their retention period ends: 30 days on the free plan, or 30/60/90 days on Glint Pro as chosen by the operator. Operators can delete photos, events or their whole account at any time.

3. Glint account (optional)

An operator can sign in with Apple to link several iPads and the Glint Remote app. We then store the name and email address Apple provides (which may be a private relay address), an account identifier, the operator's event settings (names, dates, venue, notes, themes) and the status of linked devices (booth mode, session and print counts, paper left, battery, printer name). This is used only to run the operator's booths. You can delete your account in the app, which removes your account, events, online photos, reactions, comments and guest uploads.

4. Identifiers and plan status

Each installation creates a random identifier that owns its events and online storage. The app also tells our service whether the operator is on the free plan or Glint Pro, so the right storage limit and retention apply. These identifiers are never used for advertising or tracking.

5. Purchases

Glint Pro subscriptions and the Event pass are processed by Apple. We never receive payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

6. Camera, Bluetooth and local network

The camera is used to take booth photos. Bluetooth is used only to control a ring light the operator connects. The local network is used to find printers, cameras and nearby phones. None of this data leaves the device except the photos described above.

7. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.
  • We don't use photos to train AI models.

8. Guests and consent

The booth operator is responsible for telling guests that photos are taken and shared, and for any consent required at their event (the app includes an optional consent step). Glint is not directed to children under 13.

9. Your choices and contact

Guests can ask the operator to delete their photos or details. Operators can delete data in the app or email us at essentialdevteam@gmail.com, and we will respond within 30 days.

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
